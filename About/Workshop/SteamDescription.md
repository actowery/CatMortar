[h1]Cat Mortar[/h1]

Load cats. Fire cats. Deal with the consequences.

Cat Mortar is a standalone RimWorld artillery mod for [b]1.6 and 1.7 unstable[/b]. Research Mortars, build the cat-themed mortar, designate a colony cat or cat corpse, and launch it as extremely costly artillery.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]A dedicated one-cat suspended loading chamber—no ordinary shells required.
[*]Colony cats receive a visible orange, black, cream, or brown launch coat before loading. That exact cat and coat persist through flight and survivor recovery.
[*]A 4.5-tile high-explosive primary blast with expanded vanilla-style blood and short-lived cat fragments.
[*]Orange ignites; black EMPs, obscures, and darkens; cream firefoams and stuns; brown releases a 6-tile anti-grain-style aftershock.
[*]Colony cat corpses create a weaker 3-tile explosion and toxic gas instead of a coat payload.
[*]No runtime dependencies.
[/list]

[h2]Balance[/h2]
[list]
[*]Requires Mortars research.
[*]Costs 350 steel, 25 components, and 100 chemfuel.
[*]Loading a cat is a commitment until firing or destruction of the mortar.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*]Supports RimWorld 1.6 and 1.7 unstable.
[*]Standalone companion to Cat Launcher; it does not require it.
[*]Only player-faction cats and their corpses can be loaded.
[/list]
